Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Your privacy matters.

Bubble Ball Game does not require account registration. The game developer does not directly ask you to provide personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, password, photos, or contacts. The game uses Unity Ads, which may process information through its advertising SDK as described below.

Bubble Ball Game respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

Bubble Ball Game does not directly collect personal information from users and does not require account registration.

Gameplay progress, settings, and other gameplay-related information may be stored locally on your device to support game functionality. This information is not directly transmitted to the game developer.

Third-party advertising services integrated into the game may automatically collect certain information.

2. Unity Ads Data Collection

Bubble Ball Game uses the Unity Ads SDK to provide advertising services. Depending on SDK configuration, advertising features, and applicable privacy settings, Unity Ads may collect and share certain categories of information.

Location information

  • Approximate location may be collected and shared.
  • Precise location is not collected according to the referenced Unity Ads SDK disclosure.

Personal information

  • Personal identifiers may be collected and shared.
  • Name, email address, postal address, and phone number are not collected according to the referenced disclosure.

Financial information

  • Purchase history may be collected and shared.
  • Credit card, debit card, bank account, and credit information are not collected according to the referenced disclosure.

App activity

  • Advertising-related page views and taps may be collected and shared.
  • In-app search history, installed-app information, and other user-generated content are not collected according to the referenced disclosure.

Unity Ads may also collect app usage times when the Acquire Optimization feature is enabled in the Unity Monetization Dashboard.

App diagnostics and device information

  • App diagnostics may be collected and shared.
  • Device or other identifiers may be collected and shared.
  • Crash logs and other app performance data are not collected according to the referenced disclosure.

Other information

The game does not directly request or collect health or fitness information, emails, SMS or MMS messages, photos, videos, voice recordings, music or other audio files, documents, calendar events, contacts, or web browsing history.

Third-party SDK behavior and data collection may change with SDK versions, dashboard settings, and service updates. Please verify these statements against the Unity Ads SDK version and configuration used in the published app.

3. Advertising

Bubble Ball Game uses Unity Ads to display advertisements. Unity Ads may process advertising-related information for purposes including:

  • Advertising and ad delivery
  • Advertising measurement and analytics
  • App functionality
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Legal and regulatory compliance

Read Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

4. Data Security

The game developer does not operate a system that directly collects users' personal information. Gameplay progress and settings may be stored locally on your device.

Unity describes security measures for information processed through its services, including encryption in transit, in its applicable disclosures. Third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

5. Data Deletion Requests

For applicable privacy or data deletion requests relating to Unity's services, users may contact Unity's Data Protection Officer:

Email: DPO@unity3d.com

When contacting Unity, you may mention the app name, Bubble Ball Game, and describe your privacy request. Unity may request additional information to process it.

Gameplay data stored locally can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game. This may permanently delete locally stored game progress.

More information is available in the Unity Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Bubble Ball Game does not directly request personal information from users. However, Unity Ads may process advertising-related information.

Advertising configuration, age restrictions, consent requirements, and applicable children's privacy laws must be respected. The game will follow applicable Google Play policies and relevant privacy requirements for its declared target audience.

7. Third-Party Services

The game may use the following third-party services:

  • Unity Ads: Advertising services that may collect and process advertising-related information. See the Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Android platform functionality and related services. See the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have choices regarding personalized advertising, advertising identifiers, consent to certain data processing, and applicable privacy or data deletion requests.

You can manage supported advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and available advertising privacy controls.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the game, advertising services, applicable laws, or privacy practices. Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
